# Global Employee Leave & Benefits Policy Handbook

## 1. Annual Paid Time Off (PTO)
Full-time employees receive 25 days of annual paid leave per calendar year. Up to 5 unused days may roll over into Q1 of the following year.

## 2. Sick Leave
Employees are entitled to 10 days of paid sick leave annually. Absences exceeding 3 consecutive business days require medical certification.

## 3. Parental Leave
Primary caregivers are eligible for 16 weeks of fully paid parental leave. Secondary caregivers receive 8 weeks.

## 4. Approval Workflow
All leave requests must be submitted through the internal HR portal at least two weeks in advance for planned vacation. Emergency sick leave should be logged within 24 hours of absence.
